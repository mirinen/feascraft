title: Ravager Hot Dog
description: A gloriously messy bratwurst loaded with crunch, heat and Hellfire Peninsula energy.

category: Main
prepTime: 15 minutes
cookTime: 15 minutes
servings: 2 hot dogs
date: 2026-08-16
featured: true
published: true
ingredients:
  - 2 bratwursts
  - 2 brioche hot dog buns
  - 120 g coleslaw
  - 2 tbsp crispy fried onions
  - Pickled jalapeño slices, to taste
  - 2 tbsp BBQ sauce
  - Mustard, to taste
  - 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  - 1/2 tsp smoked paprika, optional
---

1. Mix the mayonnaise and smoked paprika if you want an extra layer of smoky heat.
2. Cook the bratwursts in a pan or on a grill until browned and cooked through.
3. Lightly toast the brioche buns cut-side down.
4. Add a bratwurst to each bun, then pile on the coleslaw, pickled jalapeños and crispy fried onions.
5. Finish with BBQ sauce, mustard and the smoked paprika mayonnaise. Serve immediately, preferably before the whole thing escapes the bun.
