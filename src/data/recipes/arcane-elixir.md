ingredients:
  - 400 ml lemon or lemon-lime soda
  - 100 ml blueberry juice
  - Juice of 1/2 lime
  - 1 to 2 tsp simple syrup, optional
  - Plenty of ice
  - A few fresh mint leaves
  - A tiny pinch of edible blue or pearlescent glitter
  - Lime wedges, for serving
---
1. Fill two tall glasses generously with ice.
2. Pour the blueberry juice and lime juice into a small jug. Add simple syrup if your blueberry juice is especially tart.
3. Slowly add the soda and stir gently so the drink keeps its fizz.
4. Add a very small pinch of edible glitter. Less really is more here. A tiny amount is enough to create the swirling magical effect.
5. Divide between the glasses and finish with mint and a lime wedge.
