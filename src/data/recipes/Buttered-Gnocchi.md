Somewhere between the mismatched furniture, enchanted knitting needles and the constant chaos of the Weasley household, I imagine Molly Weasley making something like this for a quick family supper.

Nothing particularly fancy. Just golden gnocchi, plenty of butter, garlic, sage and parmesan. The sort of meal that comes together in one pan and disappears just as quickly.

And since not every evening calls for a three-hour Hogwarts feast, this one takes about 12 minutes.

## Instructions

1. Melt half the butter in a large frying pan over medium heat.
2. Add the fresh gnocchi and cook for 5–6 minutes, turning occasionally, until golden and lightly crispy.
3. Add the remaining butter, minced garlic and chopped sage.
4. Cook for about a minute, until the garlic is fragrant.
5. Add the frozen peas along with a small splash of water.
6. Cook for another 2 minutes, until the peas are heated through.
7. Stir in the grated parmesan until everything is coated in buttery goodness.
8. Season with black pepper and a little salt to taste.
9. Finish with a squeeze of lemon if you fancy a little freshness.

## A Little Kitchen Magic

The trick here is letting the gnocchi develop a golden crust before adding the rest of the ingredients. Resist the urge to stir constantly!

Fresh gnocchi works particularly well because it can go straight into the frying pan without boiling first.

Serve immediately, preferably in a big bowl with an extra sprinkle of parmesan.

No wand required.
