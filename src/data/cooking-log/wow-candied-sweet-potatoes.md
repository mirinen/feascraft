I'd never quite understood the idea of putting marshmallows on sweet potatoes, but I was bound to come across these Candied Sweet Potatoes sooner or later. They're one of the Pilgrim’s Bounty recipes in the World of Warcraft cookbook, and I'm cooking my way through the book. So, marshmallows on sweet potatoes it was.

The sweet potatoes get baked with butter, honey, brown sugar and the book's Autumnal Herbs blend. That already sounded plenty sweet to me, and then I got to the topping, a meringue made with egg whites, maple syrup and marshmallow fluff.

I think I boiled the sweet potatoes a bit too long and left the meringue in the oven a little too long as well. The little meringue clouds held their shape, though, and all that worrying about the sweetness turned out to be unnecessary. They were absolutely delicious.

The book suggests serving them with its Slow-Roasted Turkey, which is the part I'm still curious about. I haven't tried them together yet, so I'll make another batch when I cook the turkey and see how I feel about having my marshmallows with dinner.
