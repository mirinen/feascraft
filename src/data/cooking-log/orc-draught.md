Orc-draught was one of those recipes I just had to try, mostly because of the name. It sounds like something you'd be handed in a rather questionable tavern somewhere in Middle-earth, and I was curious to see what it would actually taste like.

The recipe comes from *Recipes from the World of Tolkien cookbook*, and I decided to give it a little Finnish twist by swapping the whisky for **Jaloviina Hanki**.

Despite the name, I was pleasantly surprised by how much I enjoyed it! It's a really nice drink for this time of year, when the Finnish evenings are getting colder and darkness seems to arrive earlier every day.

What started as a recipe I picked for its amusing name turned out to be one I'd happily make again.

I have to say, orcs have surprisingly good taste in drinks, although I'm basing that judgement on just one drink so far!
