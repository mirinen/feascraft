Orc-draught is one of those recipes I had to make mostly because of the name. It is green, murky and very convincing as something you might be handed somewhere you should not have stopped for a drink.

I made it from *Recipes from the World of Tolkien*. This is my cooking log rather than a copy of the recipe, so the full measurements stay in the book.
