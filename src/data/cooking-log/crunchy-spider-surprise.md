This one looks much more alarming in Azeroth than it does in my kitchen.

I used surimi for the filling and spring roll wrappers because wonton wrappers are not easy to find here. They still came out properly crisp and made a very good raid snack.

I followed the recipe from *World of Warcraft: The Official Cookbook*. I do not share the full measurements from published cookbooks, but the book is linked above if you want to make it too.
